⚔️ Vigor
What it affects:
  1. Maximum HP (health)
  2. Fire resistance
  3. Immunity resistance
Purpose: Increases your survivability. Most builds invest heavily in Vigor.

🧠 Mind
What it affects:
  1. Maximum FP (mana)
  2. Focus resistance
Purpose: Needed for casting spells, weapon skills, and Ashes of War.

💪 Endurance
What it affects:
  1. Maximum stamina
  2. Equip load (how heavy your armor and weapons can be)
  3. Robustness resistance
Purpose: Helps you roll more, block more, and wear heavier armor.

🏋️ Strength
What it affects:
  1. Physical damage with Strength-scaling weapons
  2. Ability to use heavy weapons and shields
  3. Increases damage when two-handing a weapon
Purpose: Main stat for heavy weapons (colossal swords, greataxes, hammers).

🎯 Dexterity
What it affects:
  1. Physical damage with Dexterity-scaling weapons
  2. Casting speed (slightly)
  3. Reduces falling damage
  4. Makes you harder to be knocked off your horse
Purpose: Main stat for fast weapons (katanas, curved swords, spears).

🔮 Intelligence
What it affects:
  1. Magic damage (sorceries)
  2. Magic defense
  3. Weapons that scale with Intelligence
Purpose: Main stat for sorcerers (Glintstone magic builds).

✨ Faith
What it affects:
  1. Incantation damage
  2. Ability to cast healing, fire, lightning, holy spells
  3. Increases resistance to holy damage
Purpose: Main stat for faith builds (lightning, flame, holy spells).

🎲 Arcane
What it affects:
  1. Item discovery (drop rate of items)
  2. Holy damage on certain weapons
  3. Status effects: bleed, poison, rot, frost buildup
  4. Some spells/weapons scale with Arcane
Purpose: Used for bleed builds, dragon communion spells, and luck-based weapons.
