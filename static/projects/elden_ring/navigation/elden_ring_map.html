Altus Plateau

A elevated region featuring golden fields, the royal capital Leyndell, and Mount Gelmir. The Altus Plateau serves as the gateway to the Erdtree and features diverse environments from peaceful plains to volcanic mountains.

Mountaintops of the Giants

A frozen, treacherous area high in the mountains where the Fire Giants reside. This late-game region features extreme cold, powerful enemies, and the forge where the Erdtree can be burned.

Siofra River

An mystical underground river system featuring ancient architecture, glowing flora, and ancestral spirits. This vast underground network connects to various parts of the Lands Between through wells and teleporters.

Ainsel River

Another major underground river system with dark waters and dangerous creatures. Ainsel River features unique environments and connects to important late-game areas through its depths.

Deeproot Depths

The deepest part of the underground, located beneath the Erdtree's roots. This area features giant roots, unique flora and fauna, and connections to the very foundation of the world.

Crumbling Farum Azula

A floating, crumbling city caught in an eternal storm. This ancient dragon temple exists outside of time and features some of the most challenging encounters in the game.

Miquella's Haligtree

A massive, sacred tree created by Miquella as a safe haven for those spurned by the Golden Order. This optional late-game area features unique challenges and important story elements.

Liurnia of the Lakes

A vast wetland region dominated by a massive lake, Liurnia is home to the Academy of Raya Lucaria and features shallow waters, forests, and magical environments. The region is central to many sorcery-related quests and items.

Caelid

A blighted region ravaged by Scarlet Rot, Caelid features crimson skies, mutated creatures, and dangerous swamps. This area presents a significant challenge with powerful enemies and environmental hazards around every corner.

Limgrave

Limgrave is the starting area for most Tarnished, featuring lush green fields, forests, and the iconic Church of Elleh. This region serves as an introduction to the Lands Between with relatively lower difficulty enemies and important early-game locations.
