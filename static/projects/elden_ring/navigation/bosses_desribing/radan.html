Radahn is one of the most powerful demigods and a son of Radagon and Rennala. Known as the Star Scourge, he is a legendary warrior who once stopped the stars to save the Lands Between from a cosmic catastrophe. However, the curse of Scarlet Rot has driven him insane, and he now roams the Caelid wasteland as a shadow of his former self. Radahn fights with immense strength, gravity magic, and a massive sword, reflecting his status as an unconquerable warrior. His tragic state makes him one of the most remarkable figures in the story.

Location: Starlight Wastes
Rewards: Star Scourge Greatsword; Lion Greatbow
Health: 9,572 hp
Runes: 70,000
