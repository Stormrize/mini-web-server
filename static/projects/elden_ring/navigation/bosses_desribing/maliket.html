Maliketh is the loyal guardian of the Death Rune and plays a central role in the Lands Between’s story. He embodies loyalty and duty, guarding the rune to ensure that death does not spiral out of control. Maliketh fights with a deadly black blade imbued with the power of death. In his second phase, he becomes even more aggressive, using fast and devastating attacks that keep the player constantly on the move. His defeat represents the end of an era and the loss of balance between life and death.

Location: Decaying Farum Azula
Rewards: Maliketh's Black Blade; Black Blade
Health: 10,620 hp
Runes: 220,000
