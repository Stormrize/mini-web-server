The Fire Giant is a colossal enemy guarding the Forge of Giants. He is the last of his kind, as his people were destroyed by the Golden Order. His body is scarred, and his fate is tragic. In battle, he uses massive melee attacks and powerful fire abilities, showcasing his connection to the Flame of Chaos. His downfall symbolizes the struggle between the Golden Order and the ancient forces he once sought to destroy.

Location: Summit of the Giants
Rewards: Giant Scythe; Burn, Flame!
Health: 43,263 hp
Runes: 180,000
