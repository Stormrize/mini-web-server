Rykard is a demigod and the son of Radagon and Rennala, who turned away from the Golden Order. He voluntarily merged with the great serpent, becoming the Lord of Blasphemy. Rykard rules over the Volcano Manor and leads a cult that preaches chaos and rebellion against the Order. In battle, he wields the power of the serpent, unleashing fire attacks and massive blasphemous strikes. His grotesque and powerful appearance shows how far he has gone for his ideals. Rykard represents rebellion, sacrifice, and the dark consequences of ambition.

Location: Altus Plateau
Rewards: Blasphemous Blade; Rykard's Malice
Health: 89,613 hp
Runes: 130,000
