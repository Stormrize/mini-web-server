Placidusax was once the ruler of the Age of Dragons and served as Elden Lord in a time before the Golden Order dominated the world. His body shows signs of decay and loss as he rests in a floating time capsule surrounded by storms and chaos. Placidusax is an impressive opponent, using powerful dragon attacks and lightning magic to overwhelm the player. His battle tells the story of a past era ruled by divine dragons, representing the fall of a once-mighty ruler haunted by his past.

Location: Ruined Farum Azula
Rewards: Dragonlord King's Sword; Placidusax's Wrath
Health: 26,651 hp
Runes: 280,000
