Mohg is a demigod and the twin brother of Morgott, who rejected the Golden Order and embraced the Scarlet Curse. He is the leader of a sinister cult devoted to blood rituals and seeks to create his own Tree of Men. Mohg resides deep within the Palace of the Dynasty and performs rituals to enhance his power. In combat, he uses bloody sorceries and deadly melee attacks, which are both destructive and terrifying. Mohg symbolizes rebellion and the sacrifices that lead to dark power.

Location: Siofra River
Rewards: Remembrance of the Blood Lord; Mohg's Great Rune
Health: 18,389 hp
Runes: 420,000
