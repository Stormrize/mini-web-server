Story

Elden Ring takes place in the vast and haunting world of the Lands Between, where the Elden Ring has been shattered and the demigods rule over fractured realms. Players become a Tarnished, a lost warrior called back to the world to restore the Elden Ring and claim the title of Elden Lord. Along the journey, adventurers explore sprawling open landscapes, ancient castles, hidden dungeons, and mystical forests, facing powerful bosses and uncovering the secrets and lore of a dark, magical world. The game combines challenging combat, deep RPG mechanics, and freedom to approach quests and exploration in countless ways.

Developer: FromSoftware
Publisher: Bandai Namco Entertainment
Genre: Action/RPG
Release Date: February 25, 2022
Age Rating: 16+
DLC: Shadow of the Erdtree
